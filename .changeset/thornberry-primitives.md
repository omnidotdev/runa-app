Dialogs, menus, buttons, inputs, switches, tooltips, and the pricing FAQ now use the shared Omni components, so they match the rest of Omni: menu items show a pointer, long menus and tall dialogs scroll instead of running off screen, dialogs keep a gutter on phones, and the sidebar is shared too: on a phone its drawer dims the page behind it.

Press `?` anywhere, or pick "Keyboard shortcuts" in the command palette, to see every keyboard shortcut.
